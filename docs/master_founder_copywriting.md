## 3. Individual Dashboard (Personal Branding Engine)

### 3.1. Overview & Headline
* **Section Headline:** **Your executive voice, cloned with zero artificial jargon.**
* **Section Subtext:** The Individual Dashboard is your personal creative sanctuary. Scrape your articles, podcasts, and past writings to train **Arthur**, your dedicated voice clone, ensuring every post reads like you wrote it at 6 AM.

### 3.2. Feature Copy Blocks

#### Feature 01: Founder DNA & Voice Cloning (Arthur Agent)
* **Headline:** **Extract your implicit mental models.**
* **Body Copy:** Arthur analyzes your past writing, preferred vocabulary, prohibited buzzwords, and core business philosophies. The result is a persistent voice profile that generates natural, authoritative narrative posts—eliminating generic prompt-vomit.
* **UI Microcopy:** `Calibrate Voice DNA` | `Active Profile: Founder Persona v2`

#### Feature 02: Dynamic Topic & Hook Ideation Engine
* **Headline:** **Never stare at a blank draft again.**
* **Body Copy:** Tap into real-time industry triggers, contrarian takes, tactical breakdown frameworks, and personal milestone stories. Arthur curates 5 tailored content angles every morning based on your niche.
* **UI Microcopy:** `Generate Fresh Angles` | `Topic Bucket: Market Friction & Hard Lessons`

#### Feature 03: Multi-Platform Personal Post Studio
* **Headline:** **Platform-native formatting, instantly structured.**
* **Body Copy:** Seamlessly convert one strategic idea into a high-engagement LinkedIn hook-story-lesson post, an X thread, a visual quote plate, or an Instagram text carousel with optimized line breaks and whitespace.
* **UI Microcopy:** `Preview LinkedIn Post` | `Render Visual Card` | `Copy Native Text`

#### Feature 04: Solo Content Calendar & Quick Publishing
* **Headline:** **Autopilot consistency without editorial drag.**
* **Body Copy:** Schedule your personal posts across your profile accounts or push directly with one-click dispatch. Keep your personal profile active every day while you focus on building product.
* **UI Microcopy:** `Schedule for Peak Reach` | `Push Live Now`

* **Section Subtext:** The Main Dashboard integrates your founder personal brand directly into the company’s multi-agent marketing machine. Synchronize executive posts with feature launches, market research, and multi-channel campaigns.

#### Feature 01: Dual-Track Campaign Orchestration
* **Headline:** **Align corporate messaging with founder narrative.**
* **Body Copy:** Launch 7-day or 30-day marketing campaigns where company announcements are paired with behind-the-scenes founder commentary. Drive 4x higher audience engagement by anchoring corporate product news to human leadership stories.
* **UI Microcopy:** `Synchronize Campaign` | `Track: Corporate Brand + Founder Executive`
