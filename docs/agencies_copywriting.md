### Step 01: Onboard Client Profiles (Minutes 0–5)

### Step 02: Trigger Multi-Agent Generation (Minutes 5–7)
> Select campaign focus (Thought Leadership, Feature Launch, Case Study) and activate the 10-agent network. **Sarah** fetches market trends while **Alex** and **Chloe** craft copy and visuals tailored for LinkedIn, X, Meta, and blogs.

### Step 03: Dispatch Client Approval Deck (Minutes 7–8)
