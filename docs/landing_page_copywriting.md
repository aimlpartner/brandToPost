#### 02. What happens in the first 10 minutes after I sign up?
> **Min 0-3:** Paste your site URL to extract Brand DNA and ICP objections.  
> **Min 3-8:** Review your first 30-day campaign queue.  
> **Min 8-10:** Connect your LinkedIn/X accounts and launch native autopilot publishing.

#### 03. Will this sound like robotic, generic AI slop?
> **No.** Arthur clones your exact founder personality, vocabulary constraints, and objection handlers from your Brand DNA sheet. Copy reads like a human founder wrote it, not a generic prompt box.
