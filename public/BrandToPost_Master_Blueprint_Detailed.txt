1. PRODUCT DNA & CORE VISION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
BrandToPost is an end-to-end Enterprise AI Creative Studio and distribution
engine designed to eliminate the friction between product development and native
organic post scheduling. Instead of forcing founders, marketers, and teams to
prompt blank chatbot inputs daily, BrandToPost ingests a raw product URL or 
context block and automatically extracts brand DNA, builds a multi-channel campaign, 
generates branded graphic cards, and distributes content natively via direct APIs.

Key Value Pillars:
* ZERO-PROMPT DISTRIBUTION: Paste a URL, approve the campaign, and watch it deploy.
* AGENT ORCHESTRATION (TROR): Specialized AI agents handle focused steps of the process:
  - Arthur: Voice alignment, brand DNA extraction, psychographic mapping.
  - Sarah: Web researcher, scrapes industry context, targets triggers.
  - Alex: Multi-platform copywriting agent, drafts posts natively.
  - Chloe & Julian: Graphics, asset layout, typography card generators.
  - Max: Publishing specialist, handles native API distribution.
* ART BOOK PRESENTATION: A premium, minimal, editorial design language that rejects 
  standard SaaS layouts (no generic card borders, no shadows, no standard dashboards).

2. DESIGN SYSTEM & VISUAL SPECS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The design system of BrandToPost is inspired by high-end luxury catalogs, art gallery
exhibits, and professional print journals. It relies on clean dividers, massive display
typography, generous whitespace, and a high contrast between dark focus areas and light
editorial canvases.

THEME SPECIFICATIONS:
* Overall Aesthetic: "Editorial Luxury Tech" (Glassmorphism, thin rules, vast paddings)
* Primary Background (Light Canvas): #FAF9F6 (Warm off-white, soft plaster color)
* Primary Background (Dark Canvas): #08080C (Deep absolute black with purple hues)
* Card Backgrounds:
  - Light Theme: White (#FFFFFF) with 5% alpha border rules.
  - Dark Theme: Translucent Glass (rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)) with backdrop-blur.
* Divider Rules:
  - Light Theme: border-slate-900/10 or border-slate-200/60
  - Dark Theme: border-white/5 or border-white/10

TYPOGRAPHY & FONTS:
* Heading Font: "Outfit" (Google Fonts)
  - Characterized by clean, modern, slightly rounded geometric structures.
  - Applied using `.font-display` class.
  - Used for large display titles (`text-4xl` up to `text-7xl` or `text-8xl`), thin weight (`font-light`), and italic highlights.
* Body Sans Font: "Inter" (Google Fonts)
  - Sleek, highly legible, optimized for screen readability.
  - Used for standard paragraphs, descriptions, and user inputs.
* Technical/Monospace Font: "Space Grotesk" or standard system monospace
  - Used for tags, badges, step counts, console logs, and system metrics.

COLOR TOKENS & HEX CODES:
* Warm Off-White (Canvas): #FAF9F6
* Deep Slate Black (Dark Text/Dark Canvas): #08080C
* Primary Brand Purple (Voice Highlight): #7C3AED
* Light Brand Purple (Glows/Gradients): #C084FC
* Success Brand Green (Metrics/Verified): #10B981
* Process Brand Blue (Research/Data): #2583EB
* Warning Brand Pink (Constraints/Errors): #F43F5E
* Subtle Slate Gray (Muted Text/Secondary): #64748B
* Border Outlines: rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.6) (Light) / rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05) (Dark)

4. ACTIVE AGENTS DEFINITIONS & PROFILES
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. ARTHUR (Brand Architect Agent)
   - Profile: "The strategic brain." Arthur reads raw product details, analyzes URLs, 
     objections, and locks down the brand voice guideline file.
   - Outputs: Strategic DNA manuals, whitelist vocabulary rules, core positioning hooks.

2. SARAH (Market Researcher Agent)
   - Profile: "The intelligence gathering grid." Sarah scrapes web urls, reads competitor 
     updates, and monitors active industry topics.
   - Outputs: Industry context decks, trend logs, marketing triggers.

3. ALEX (Platform Copywriter Agent)
   - Profile: "The native translator." Alex takes DNA rules and writes native posts.
   - Outputs: Paragraph layouts for LinkedIn, thread scripts for X, stories for Reddit.

4. CHLOE & JULIAN (Creative Overlay Directors)
   - Profile: "The visual overlay agents." They isolate product graphics, remove backgrounds, 
     place products into luxurious environments, and apply stylized typography.
   - Outputs: Data-infographics, powerful quote visual assets.

5. MAX (Publishing Coordinator Agent)
   - Profile: "The network operator." Max schedules and executes publishing scripts natively.
   - Outputs: Direct API post requests, live publication schedules, tracking logs.

6. TROR (The Master Orchestrator Agent)
   - Profile: "The central nervous system." TROR routes inputs to Arthur, passes research to 
     Sarah, assigns copy tasks to Alex, and coordinates Chloe & Julian for graphics.
   - Outputs: Orchestration loops, active pipeline telemetry logs.
