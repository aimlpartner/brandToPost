========================================================================
       BRANDTOPOST: KEYNOTE / LINEAR-STYLE HYPERFRAMES SCRIPT
========================================================================
Target Length: 90 Seconds
Style: Premium Startup (Stripe/Linear/Apple Keynote)
Core Ethos: "Show, don't tell." Extreme minimalism, high contrast, typography-led. 
Vibe: Deep focus, engineered luxury, zero-friction. 

========================================================================
▶ ANIMATION RULES (For Hyperframes / GSAP instructions):
========================================================================
1. NO GENERIC FADES. Elements do not simply fade in (opacity: 0 to 1). 
2. USE TEXT MASKING: All text must be inside an `overflow: hidden` wrapper. The text translates from `y: 100%` up to `y: 0%` using `ease: "power4.out"`. This creates the signature "reveal from nothing" Apple effect.
3. MOTION PHYSICS: Use tight, premium easing. `expo.out` for fast, snappy slides. `power3.inOut` for fluid, deliberate camera pans. 
4. UI DEPTH: No flat UI. Mockups must have `transformPerspective` applied, using subtle 3D rotations (e.g., `rotateX: 10`, `rotateY: -15`). Show the layers.
5. LIGHTING: Use CSS radial-gradients to create subtle spotlights tracking across dark backgrounds. 

========================================================================
🎨 DESIGN SYSTEM
========================================================================
Backgrounds: Void Black (#0A0A0F) and dark glassmorphism.
Typography: "Outfit" (Headings), "Inter" (UI details). Both strictly white (#FFFFFF) or muted gray (#A1A1AA).
Accents: Brand Purple (#7C3AED) used strictly for glowing highlights, and Brand Green (#18F07A) for completion states.

========================================================================
🎬 SCRIPT & SCENE DIRECTIVES
========================================================================

--- SCENE 1: THE VOID (0:00 - 0:10) ---
Visual: Pitch black. Silent. 
Motion: A single horizontal 1px line of light (#7C3AED) traces across the screen from left to right. It expands vertically into a completely blurred, frosted glass rectangle.
Text Reveals (Masked Y-translation, perfectly staggered):
"Creative production"
"is broken."

--- SCENE 2: THE FRICTION (0:10 - 0:22) ---
Visual: The glass rectangle fractures. We see beautifully rendered Pill-shapes (representing tools) floating in an isometric 3D grid. 
Text on Pills: "Briefs", "Slack", "Figma", "Spreadsheets".
Motion: They slide against each other clumsily, creating "friction" (subtle shake animations). 
Text Reveals (Minimal & Sharp):
"Too many tools." (Pause)
"Too much noise." (Pause)

--- SCENE 3: THE PARADIGM SHIFT (0:22 - 0:35) ---
Visual: The noisy pills are instantly sucked into a singularity at the center of the screen. Absolute silence. 
Motion: A pulse of deep purple/blue gradient light expands from the center. Out of it, the BrandToPost 'B' logo reconstructs itself piece by piece using slow, elegant spring physics (`back.out(1.2)`).
Text Reveals (Massive Outfit Font): 
"BrandToPost."
"Your Enterprise AI Creative Studio."

--- SCENE 4: 1. PRODUCT DNA (0:35 - 0:48) ---
Visual: The environment shifts to a sleek, 3D diagonal isometric view. A translucent dashboard softly slides up from the bottom edge.
Text Reveals: "Step 1: Product DNA."
Motion: We see lines of code/data rapidly typing out natively inside the Glass Card. 
Lines appearing: 
> Target ICP: Tech CMOs
> Enemy: Wasted Spend
> Tone: Visionary
> Hex: #7C3AED
Concept: The visual proves that the AI actually understands the core brand strategy before generating anything.

--- SCENE 5: 2. CAMPAIGN MATRICES (0:48 - 0:58) ---
Visual: The single DNA card snaps backward in Z-space and multiplies exponentially. 
Motion: 7 glowing cards fan out gracefully like a deck of premium playing cards. 
Text Reveals: "Step 2: 7-Day Campaign Matrices."
Subtext: "Zero prompts. Perfect strategy."
Visual Detail: Small icons inside the cards light up dynamically—LinkedIn, X, Instagram—showing cross-platform tailoring.

--- SCENE 6: 3. THE STUDIO ENGINE (0:58 - 0:75) ---
Visual: The cards clear. A raw photograph of a product (e.g., a headphone) drops into the center. 
Text Reveals: "Step 3: Studio Production."
Motion Sequence (THE 'WOW' FACTOR):
1. A crisp, glowing #18F07A (Green) laser line sweeps down the image. 
2. The messy background is instantly wiped to a pure transparent alpha grid. 
3. The background seamlessly morphs into an ultra-luxurious, dark 3D environment.
4. Typography (in the BrandToPost Outfit font) slides forcefully into place over the image, perfectly aligned. 
Text Reveals: "Automated extraction." "AI environments." "Brand-locked overlays."

--- SCENE 7: THE FLIGHT PATH (0:75 - 0:83) ---
Visual: Zoom out to macro level. We see the finished creative locked behind a glass card.
Text Reveals: "Step 4: Review & Deploy."
Motion: The card shrinks slightly. A UI cursor appears, clicks an "Approve" button. 
Data streams (beautiful glowing lines) shoot out from the card connecting to nodes labeled by social networks. 

--- SCENE 8: THE FINALE (0:83 - 0:90) ---
Visual: Everything collapses back into the sheer, pitch-black void.
Text reveals in massive pure white typography:
"An elite creative agency."
"Inside one intelligent agent."

Motion: The BrandToPost Logo pulses once softly in the center. 
Below it, a perfectly manicured 1px border button fades up smoothly:
"[ Experience BrandToPost ]"

(Fade to black)
