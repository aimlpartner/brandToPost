========================================================================
           BRANDTOPOST: THE "HYPERFRAMES" CALIBER VIDEO SCRIPT
========================================================================
Target Length: 90 Seconds
Vibe: High-paced, sleek, mildly provocative (like the Trellis ad), luxurious tech, Glassmorphism.
Color Palette to apply in Hyperframes:
- Base Backgrounds: #0A0A0F and #1C1C22
- Primary Accents: #7C3AED (Purple) and #2583EB (Blue)
- Text Colors: #F7F7F8 (White/Light) and #A1A1AA (Muted Gray)
- UI Elements: Translucent glass blocks with thin #7C3AED borders and glowing dropshadows.
Fonts to apply in Hyperframes: 
- Outfit (For massive, punchy Headlines)
- Inter (For small body/UI text)
