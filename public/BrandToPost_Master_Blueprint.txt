1. VISION & PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: To eliminate the friction between creative ideation and campaign deployment. BrandToPost is an end-to-end Enterprise AI Creative Studio designed to transform pure product data or rough concepts into polished, platform-tailored, and visually stunning campaigns in minutes.
Vision: To give every business the power of an elite marketing agency and creative studio entirely driven by an intelligent, autonomous AI Agent.

2. THE MASCOT & PERSONA
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mascot: "The B2P Agent" — An autonomous, sharp, and highly capable AI director. It handles research, editing, copywriting, and layout. It's represented simply but elegantly through the BrandToPost Iconography (the bold "B" in the Purple/Blue gradient).
Persona: Bold, luxurious yet deeply technical, hyper-efficient, and brutally honest about creative quality. 

3. BRAND IDENTITY (COLORS & FONTS)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fonts:
- Primary Display (Headings): "Outfit" - Clean, modern, tech-forward.
- Primary Sans (Body): "Inter" - Highly legible, sleek, minimal.

Color Palette (Creative Luxury Tech):
- Primary Background: Dark 1 (#0A0A0F) & Dark 2 (#1C1C22)
- Text/Light Elements: Light (#F7F7F8)
- Brand Purple: #7C3AED (Core identity, gradients, and glows)
- Brand Blue: #2583EB (Used alongside purple for primary gradients)
- Brand Green: #18F07A (Success states, growth metrics)
- Brand Pink: #FF7778 (Accents, error states, human elements)

UI Styling / Architecture:
- Theme: "Glassmorphism Tech Luxury"
- Cards use frosted glass (backdrop-filter: blur), subtle inset shadows, and glowing purple/blue gradient borders.
- Animations feature slow gradient-X pulses and shimmering borders.

========================================================================
                  THE CORE FEATURES & WORKFLOW
========================================================================

PHASE 1: PRODUCT DNA (The Foundation)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before generating anything, the AI learns exactly who you are. This isn't just about uploading a logo; it's deep strategic alignment.

- Core Details: Name, Website, Tone, Stage.
- Target Audience & Positioning: Defines exactly who the product is talking to.
- Visual Data / Visual Brand Rules: Enforces your specific color hexes, typography hierarchy, primary/secondary fonts, and specific image styles. Appends Dark/Light mode logos.
- Advanced Psychographics: The AI calculates your buyer's "Enemy," "Hell State," and "Heaven State." Identifies objections, the "Earned Secret," origin story, proof points, and strict Vocabulary rules (words to always use vs. words to avoid).
- Strategy Output: Translates the DNA into Content Pillars, ICPs (Ideal Customer Profiles) with distinct pain points, and Recommended Themes.

PHASE 2: INTELLIGENT CAMPAIGN GENERATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Takes the Product DNA and spins up an entire week-long campaign fully automatically.

- Research Module: Pulls real market data/summaries to anchor the campaign in reality.
- Campaign Matrix: Determines the Theme, Core Message, Hook, Call-to-Action (CTA), Content Format, Sub Category, and Confidence Score.
- 7-Day Matrix Generation: Creates distinct Daily Posts (Monday to Sunday).
- Cross-Platform Tailoring: Automatically tailors EVERY daily post specifically for LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Instagram, Facebook, and Reddit. Adapting tone, length, and format natively to the platform.

PHASE 3: AI CREATIVE STUDIO & VISUAL GENERATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
BrandToPost is an integrated design studio, not just a text generator.

- Automated Prompts: Generates exact AI Image Prompts to accompany daily posts. 
- Background Removal & Environment Placement: (Core Workflow) Takes a raw product image, strips the background, places it in a luxurious AI environment, and matches the lighting.
- Intelligent Visual Overlays: Instead of bare images, it overlays text based on the content type:
   -> Data-Infographics (Stats, labels)
   -> Creative-Story (Headlines, subtext)
   -> Powerful-Quote (Stylized typography)
   -> Abstract-Announcement

PHASE 4: COLLABORATION & FEEDBACK LOOP
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Built for teams and agencies to easily approve and refine content.

- Shared Campaigns: Generates live, shareable links. Reviewers, stakeholders, or clients can view the entire campaign structure.
- Micro-Feedback System: Reviewers can leave comments on specific posts or days.
- Automated Refinement: The system uses AI to process the feedback and automatically improve/rewrite the posts (flagged with `improvedViaFeedback: true`).

PHASE 5: SCHEDULING & AUTO-POSTING (THE FINAL MILE)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Centralized Calendar: A comprehensive schedule view mapping out the generated creatives.
- Distribution: Direct pipeline to deploy these perfectly branded, multi-platform posts out to X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, etc., automatically based on the chosen dates and times.
- Integrations: Easily hooks into existing workflows via Google Drive for asset management and direct API connections for social platforms.

SUMMARY OF THE END-TO-END WORKFLOW:
1. You input raw product details.
2. The AI calculates deep Product DNA and visual rules.
3. The AI orchestrates a multi-channel, multi-day campaign structure.
4. The AI directs, edits, and produces the visual creatives (removing BGs, adding environments, overlaying text).
5. Stakeholders review via a shared link; the AI auto-edits based on feedback.
6. The campaign automatically deploys to social platforms through the Scheduler.

This is BrandToPost: The complete operating system for modern creatives and growth marketers.
